## Wireframe 1: Course Tracking (My Courses)

**Designed by:** Alston Dsouza

**Figma Wireframe:** [View Course Tracking Wireframe](https://www.figma.com/design/C9ovS8MvoGZD6q0EadFonn/StudySync---Course-Tracking-Wireframe?node-id=0-1)

### Description

The Course Tracking (My Courses) page allows students to view and organize their enrolled courses, monitor assignment completion, and keep track of upcoming deadlines in one centralized location.

### Key Features

- **Navigation Bar:** Provides access to Dashboard, Assignments, Courses, and Study Plans.
- **Course Search:** Allows students to search for courses by name or course code.
- **Course Cards:** Displays individual courses with their names, assignment completion progress, and upcoming deadlines.
- **Add Course Button:** Provides an entry point for adding courses.
- **View Course Button:** Allows students to navigate to individual course details.
- **Assignment Progress:** Displays completion percentages to help students track their academic progress.
- **Upcoming Deadlines:** Highlights approaching assignment deadlines for each course.

### Purpose

The purpose of this page is to help students stay organized by providing a clear overview of their courses and academic responsibilities. Students can quickly identify upcoming deadlines, monitor their progress, and navigate between courses.
